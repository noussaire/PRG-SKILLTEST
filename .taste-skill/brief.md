# SkillQuest — redesign brief and decisions

## Subject inventory

- Chaba Connect is the association behind the platform.
- The provided logo combines an academic cap, a globe/network and blue/green lettering.
- SkillQuest means a journey through skills; the route itself is the subject.
- Exactly 31 soft skills are explored once each.
- Four actions are available in each situation.
- Prepared consequences make the following question depend on the previous decision.
- French and Arabic are equally functional, including reports and RTL.
- The core path makes no external AI request; the immediate report is always available.
- Optional AI enrichment is queued after completion, never required to keep answering.
- The participant supplies a full name and email without creating an account.
- The real output is a saved, printable report with contextual indicators and advice.
- The second test, égogramme, remains unavailable; no invented launch date.

## Argument and copy deck

Audience: adult students exploring their decisions, and organisers following their participation.
First-screen belief: the participant's own choices drive the path. Next action: start the existing test.

- Hero: “Vos choix, votre voie.” / “اختياراتك، طريقك.” Proof: real interface views in a moving reel.
- Intro: “Essayez un choix.” Proof: the actual first question, four public options and their saved consequences. The demo never saves or grades the visitor.
- Report: “Un bilan qui vous sert.” Proof: real rendered report preview and the actual output fields.
- Index: “31 compétences, un parcours.” Proof: the complete public catalog and Arabic report preview.
- Questions: six precise answers about availability, branching, pause, data access, indicators and égogramme.

## Direction and implementation

Taste direction: `sentence-as-layout`, seed 1954362474. Chosen because the two halves of a sentence can sit around the material generated by the participant's choices. No redraw of the direction, palette or type; original blue/green brand and FR/AR faces are retained. The small media reel uses actual interface captures rather than unrelated stock photography.

Idea: on this page, the decisions and saved report of the SkillQuest journey become the missing material between “Vos choix” and “votre voie”. Live thing: select one of four actual actions and see its real consequence and follow-up question. No provider calls or personal data in the demo.

App direction: `framed-sheet`, seed 1997044426. A pale blue-green frame holds navigation and a white working sheet. Its face is the SkillQuest logo and the brand blue on the real skill indicators. Main question: how many participants finished, and which reports can the organiser read? The four metrics are derived from the existing API, not invented. The queue status and recent participants make the next action visible.

## Before critique

The old page: long hero at three lines on mobile, accent word, eyebrow, soft gradient, shadowed static UI, repeated icon cards. Motion count 0. The rounded KPI tiles and marketing-like dashboard header prioritised chrome over participant data.

## Asset provenance

The four preview PNGs are captured from actual SkillQuest components with local example choices and no external AI calls. They contain a demonstration identity, not real participant data. No found photos, third-party stock imagery, paid libraries or fabricated testimonials are used. The official supplied logo remains unchanged.
